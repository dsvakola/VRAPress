# VRAPress Product Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Product foundation

- Preserve and tag the `1.0.0` baseline
- Establish version control, coding standards, issue register, decision log, and test strategy
- Separate local secrets and runtime data from distributable source

## Phase 2 — Complete technical audit

- Audit architecture, database, installer, routing, configuration, dependencies, and hosting assumptions
- Record defects by severity, impact, reproduction steps, and acceptance criteria

## Phase 3 — Core repair and security hardening

- Strengthen authentication, authorization, sessions, CSRF, validation, sanitization, uploads, errors, and database integrity

## Phase 4 — Content-management reliability

- Stabilize pages, posts, categories, comments, menus, media, editor, drafts, scheduling, previews, revisions, autosave, and recovery

## Phase 5 — Product foundations

- Add themes, extension hooks, roles and permissions, migrations, updates, backups, import/export, SEO, caching, and scheduled tasks

## Phase 6 — Professional experience

- Improve responsive administration, accessibility, search, navigation, dashboards, notifications, and public presentation

## Phase 7 — Quality gates

- Add automated functional, security, migration, browser, accessibility, performance, clean-install, and upgrade testing

## Phase 8 — Release preparation

- Complete documentation, deployment profiles, recovery procedures, licensing, branding, versioning, and staging validation

## Phase 9 — Production readiness

- Conduct the final audit, controlled deployment, monitoring setup, and post-release maintenance programme

## Change-control rule

New ideas are assessed against the product goals, assigned to the appropriate phase, documented with acceptance criteria, and regression-tested before release.
